About Ian Kimemia

Rooted in the breathtaking landscapes of Kutus, Kirinyaga, Ian Kimemia is an accomplished contemporary visual artist whose creative compass bridges traditional fine art disciplines and modern illustrative cultures.

Ian's work in Charcoal explores the human condition, African heritage, and untamed wildlife with startling hyper-realistic depth and chiaroscuro tonal control. In his Paintings, rich impasto and luminous glazes capture the vitality of Kenya’s natural vistas and urban rhythms. Meanwhile, his Anime Art channels cinematic energy, combining dynamic Japanese animation aesthetics with bold original storytelling.

Charcoal Realism

Fine-grain graphite, powdered charcoal, and dramatic contrast on archival paper.

Vibrant Paints

Heavy body acrylics, oils, and gold-leaf textures on custom stretched linen.

Anime & Concept Art

Stylized character design, action dynamics, and high-impact digital/print works.
